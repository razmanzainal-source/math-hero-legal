Privacy Policy / Dasar Privasi

Effective date: 3 November 2026

Math Hero respects the privacy of children and families. This policy explains what information the app stores and how it is used.

1. Information stored on your device

Math Hero stores app settings, practice progress, scores, battle history, badges and an optional player name locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer.

2. Permissions

  • Local Network: used only when two devices join the same Wi-Fi for LAN battles. No internet server is used for battles.
  • Notifications: used only for local practice reminders scheduled on the device.
  • Math Hero does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location.

3. Optional parent reports

Parents or guardians can choose to send a practice summary to their own Telegram bot or WhatsApp account. This is optional and is configured behind the parent settings PIN. The summary may include the selected year, number of questions completed, attempts and weak steps, plus the optional player name. When enabled, the summary is sent directly to Telegram or WhatsApp using the parent's own credentials; it is not received by the developer. Telegram and WhatsApp have their own privacy policies.

4. No advertising, analytics or accounts

Math Hero does not contain advertising, in-app purchases, accounts, analytics or tracking SDKs. The developer does not collect personal data from the app.

5. Children's privacy

Math Hero is designed for children to use with parent or guardian supervision. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parent settings are protected by a PIN. If a parent configures optional reports, the parent controls the destination and can stop them at any time.

6. Data deletion

Uninstalling Math Hero removes the app's local data from the device. To delete report messages, delete them in Telegram or WhatsApp. To stop reports, turn them off in parent settings.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
